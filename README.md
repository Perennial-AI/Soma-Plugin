# Soma for Claude

Soma is the organizational thinking partner from Perennial, purpose-built for the frontier of leadership. She knows your organization: the people, the teams, the decisions, and what has worked before. Behind that sits a deep library of organizational psychology, group dynamics and facilitation practice. Leaders use Soma to think through a hard conversation, prepare a meeting, read a team's dynamics, or make sense of what their workforce told her in sessions.

This plugin brings Soma into Claude.

## Install

In Claude, add Soma from the plugin directory. In Claude Code:

```
/plugin marketplace add Perennial-AI/Soma-Plugin
/plugin install soma@soma-plugin
```

## Use it

Connect Soma once from the plugin's Connectors tab, signing in with your Soma account and choosing the workspace Claude may use. Then:

- **Ask Soma** (`ask`). Claude relays your question and carries the conversation on, with Soma's context and pushback.
- **Brief Soma** (`brief`). Tell her what you shipped, decided or are struggling with. She remembers it in the app and in Claude.
- **Build with Soma** (`deck`, `memo`). Decks, memos and documents for others: Claude writes, Soma supplies what she knows about your organization.
- **Hand things over** (`import`). Meeting notes, documents and session output go to Soma to keep in your workspace.

## Data

Every conversation Claude has with Soma appears in your Soma workspace, streamed live, where you can continue, edit or delete it. Sign in with your existing Soma account and choose the workspace Claude may use. The plugin stores nothing itself.
